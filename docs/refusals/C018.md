# C018

- **Family:** C -- connections, settings and stores
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/profiles.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/runtime/test/sabotage-profiles.test.ts` (`C018 a connection standing in for itself, or for one of another kind; R001 one that names nothing`) and `packages/runtime/test/sabotage-broker-profile.test.ts` (a broker standing in for a connection that delivers nothing, or delivers otherwise)

## Refuses when

A profile's `connections` maps a connection the documents name to another that stands in for it under that profile,
and the mapping is no stand-in: the connection is mapped to itself, or to a connection of a different kind. A
connection's kind is a fact about the connection, and every binding and graph that reaches it was checked against
that kind; reaching a system of another kind takes other operations, which is a different binding, not a different
connection. The refusal points at `profiles/<name>/connections`. A connection on either side that the tree does not
have is R001.

A connection that is a broker and nothing else is the one exception. Where the replaced connection's kind declares
`delivery` (RFC 0009) and neither `storage` nor `leases`, a stand-in of another kind that declares the same
`delivery` is admitted: what was judged of a connection a queue trigger receives from or a graph publishes to
reads its kind's `delivery` (T009, T010, X402), which the two share, or its `storage` marker (X405), which a
stand-in marked `storage` can only relax. So a profile may swap the in-process broker for the table one, as it
swaps one storage engine's connection for another's. The replaced kind must be a broker alone because a store
and a lease judge the connection they name as written (X203, X254): a store's connection stood in for by a
broker that keeps no store would pass them and fail every store call. A broker mapped to a connection that
delivers nothing, or delivers otherwise (`at-most-once` for `at-least-once`), is still refused, as is a broker
of another kind standing in for a connection marked `storage` or `leases`, and the message says why.

## Example

Map the employees connection to itself under `production`:

```
C018  @project.json#profiles/production/connections
    profile 'production' maps '@connections/employees.connection.json' to itself
    → a stand-in is another connection of kind '@auth/directory.connection-kind.json'; to reach a different kind, bind the port to another binding
```

Mapped to a connection of another kind, the message names both: `profile 'production' maps
'@connections/employees.connection.json', of kind '@auth/directory.connection-kind.json', to
'@connections/customers-api.connection.json', of kind '@http/http.connection-kind.json'`.

Mapped from a broker to a connection that delivers nothing, the message says so: `profile 'production' maps
'@connections/jobs.connection.json', of kind '@queue-memory/memory.connection-kind.json', to
'@connections/customers-api.connection.json', of kind '@http/http.connection-kind.json' (which delivers nothing,
where the other delivers at-least-once)`. Mapped from the store's connection to a broker of another kind, it says
the replaced kind is more than a broker: `profile 'production' maps
'@connections/customers-postgres.connection.json', of kind '@storage-postgres/postgres.connection-kind.json', to
'@connections/jobs.connection.json', of kind '@queue-memory/memory.connection-kind.json' (only a broker that is
nothing else may be stood in for by another kind, and this kind is marked storage and leases too)`.

## Fix

Name another connection of the same kind as the stand-in -- or, for a connection that is a broker and nothing
else, one of any kind that declares the same `delivery` -- or drop the entry if the profile should reach the
connection itself. If the profile should reach a system of another kind, leave `connections` alone and choose,
under the profile's `bindings`, a binding of the port written against that kind.

## History

- 0.1.0: introduced.
- 0.1.0, RFC 0009 step 10: a stand-in of another kind is admitted where the replaced kind declares `delivery` and
  neither `storage` nor `leases`, and the stand-in declares the same `delivery`, so a profile can move a queue from
  one broker to another.
