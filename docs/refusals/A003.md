# A003

- **Family:** A -- access
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/access.ts`
- **Proved by:** `libraries/access/test/access.test.ts` (`A003 an outcome nothing the decision reaches refuses with`)

## Refuses when

A policy's `outcomes` maps a reason that nothing its decision reaches refuses with, under any profile. It is T006 for
a policy: a mapping nothing reaches is a misspelling or a leftover, and it tells a reader that the policy can answer
in a way it cannot. The refusal sits at the outcome, `outcomes/<reason>`.

## Example

Add a `teapot` outcome to `signed-in`:

```
A003  @features/access/edge/signed-in.policy.json#outcomes/teapot
    outcomes maps reason 'teapot', but nothing '@access/domain/access.port.json#requireSignedIn' reaches refuses with it
    → remove it, or spell the reason the way the graph does
```

## Fix

Remove the outcome, or spell it the way the graph behind the decision spells its `refuse`.
