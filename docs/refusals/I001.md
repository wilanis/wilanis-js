# I001

- **Family:** I -- invariants
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/invariants.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/runtime/test/sabotage-invariants.test.ts` (`I001 a trigger reaching a gated operation without the policy the invariant names`, `I001 the proves form: a path none of the policies a reaching trigger attaches establishes`)

## Refuses when

A trigger reaches, under some profile, a domain operation an access invariant lists under `over`, and the policies
it attaches do not meet the invariant's `requires`. Two forms: `requires.policy` names a policy the trigger does not
attach (`gates with <policy>, but attaches no such policy`), or `requires.proves` names a path, such as
`request.principal`, that no attached policy lists in its `proves` or proves a path above (`requires "<path>"
proved, but attaches no policy that proves it`). Reaching is transitive: a trigger that fires one operation whose
graph calls a covered one is refused too, and the message says `reached through <op>`. The refusal is against the
trigger, at `policies`. A policy or a path the invariant names wrongly is refused against the invariant instead
(R001, I002), never here.

## Example

Take the registrar policy off the delete-customer trigger:

```
I001  @features/customers/edge/delete-customer.trigger.json#policies
    trigger reaches @features/customers/domain/customer.port.json#remove, which 'Writes are for registrars' (@features/customers/domain/writes-are-for-registrars.invariant.json) gates with @access/edge/can-register.policy.json, but attaches no such policy
    → attach "@access/edge/can-register.policy.json" under policies, or take @features/customers/domain/customer.port.json#remove out of the invariant's over
```

## Fix

Attach the policy under the trigger's `policies`, or, for the proves form, a policy whose `proves` lists the path,
giving the guard its credentials (`in`) where no earlier policy of the trigger does. The hint's second edit, taking
the operation out of the invariant's `over`, is right only when the rule itself was wrong; it is never the way to
quiet a way in that should be gated.
