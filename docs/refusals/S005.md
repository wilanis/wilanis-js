# S005

- **Family:** S -- scenarios
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/scenarios.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/runtime/test/sabotage-scenarios.test.ts` (`refuses a policy the trigger does not attach, at
  policy, naming what it attaches`, `refuses a policy on a trigger that attaches none`, `refuses a policy the tree does
  not have, at policy`)

## Refuses when

A scenario's `policy` names no policy document, or one its `trigger` does not attach under `policies`. A policy
scenario replays the policy's `decide` under the trigger's kind and settings, the way `wilanis rehearse` walks a
policy's decision, so the trigger must be one the policy gates. The two are matched by canonical path, as the
rehearsal matches a policy to the triggers that attach it, so `@access/edge/x.policy.json` and
`@features/access/edge/x.policy.json` are one policy. The message lists what the trigger attaches. A trigger the tree
does not have is S001's, and the attachment is not judged beside it. The refusal sits at `policy`.

## Example

```
S005  @scenarios/rehearsed/get-customer/customers.get-row.outcome.noCustomer.scenario.json#policy
    trigger @features/customers/edge/delete-customer.trigger.json does not attach policy @features/access/edge/signed-in.policy.json: it attaches @features/access/edge/employees-only.policy.json, @features/access/edge/can-register.policy.json
    → a policy scenario replays the decision under a trigger that attaches it: wilanis describe @features/customers/edge/delete-customer.trigger.json
```

## Fix

Point `trigger` at a trigger that attaches the policy (`wilanis describe <trigger>` lists a trigger's policies), or
`policy` at one the trigger attaches. A recorded scenario is regenerated with `wilanis rehearse --record` rather than
edited. If the trigger no longer attaches the policy on purpose, delete the scenario.
