# T006

- **Family:** T -- triggers
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/triggers.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/runtime/test/sabotage.test.ts` (`T006 a mapped reason nothing the trigger fires refuses with`)

## Refuses when

A trigger's refusal table maps a reason that nothing it fires, no policy it attaches, and not the guard can refuse
with, under any profile that serves the trigger (A006 says which): a reason only a profile that never opens it would
reach is not reached. A mapping nothing reaches is a misspelling of one that is reached or a leftover of a graph
that changed, and either way it tells a reader of the trigger something false. The refusal sits at the mapping
itself, `settings/<table>/<reason>`. The same holds of `invariant`: it is reached only where the compiler lowers a
guard on the way, and a site the checker proves adds none.

## Example

Map `teapot` on the get route:

```
T006  @features/customers/edge/get-customer.trigger.json#settings/response/refusals/teapot
    settings.response.refusals maps reason 'teapot', but nothing this trigger fires, gates on or identifies with refuses with it
    → remove it, or spell the reason the way the graph does
```

## Fix

Remove the mapping, or spell it the way the document that refuses spells it. Dropping the last policy of a trigger
leaves the guard's reasons (`anonymous`, `invalid_credential`) mapped with nothing to reach them, and they go too.
