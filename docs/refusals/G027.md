# G027

- **Family:** G -- graphs
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/attempts.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/runtime/test/sabotage-attempts.test.ts` (`G027 a timeout or a backoff longer than a Node
  timer can wait, which Node would fire after 1 ms`); `packages/runtime/test/attempts.test.ts` (`waits at most
  TIMER_MAX_MS, rather than the 1 ms Node takes a longer timer for`), for the doubled backoff

## Refuses when

A call site waits on a timer longer than a Node timer can wait. A data graph's `run` or `map` node, and a binding's
operation, may say `timeoutMs` (the most the call may take) and `retry.backoffMs` (the wait before the second try).
Each is handed to `setTimeout`, and Node fires a timer set above 2147483647 ms (2^31 - 1, about 24.8 days) after 1 ms.
So a `timeoutMs` above that would time out every try at once, and a `backoffMs` above it would try the call again at
once. The refusal sits at `nodes/<id>/timeoutMs` or `operations/<name>/timeoutMs`, and at `.../retry/backoffMs`.

A backoff doubles before each further try, so `backoffMs: 1000` with `times: 23` would ask for 1000 * 2^22 ms before
the last try. The rule does not refuse that: the retry wrapper waits at most 2147483647 ms between two tries, so a
doubled wait stops growing at the bound. A domain graph that says either word is refused by L012 instead.

## Example

Give get-row's `fetched` node a timeout of 30 days:

```
G027  @features/customers/data/get-row.graph.json#nodes/fetched/timeoutMs
    timeoutMs is 2592000000; it is the most this call may take, in milliseconds, and a Node timer waits at most 2147483647 ms (about 24.8 days): a longer one fires after 1 ms, so every try would time out at once
    → set it to 2147483647 or less, or drop it for no limit
```

A `backoffMs` of 30 days is refused at `nodes/fetched/retry/backoffMs`, saying `so the call would be tried again at
once`, with the hint `set it to 2147483647 or less, or drop it for no wait`.

## Fix

Write a number of 2147483647 or less. Drop `timeoutMs` to let the call wait as long as its handler does, or drop
`backoffMs` to try again at once.
