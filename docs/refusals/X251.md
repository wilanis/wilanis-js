# X251

- **Family:** X -- plugin rules, @schedule (@wilanis/plugin-schedule)
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/plugin-schedule/src/rules.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/plugin-schedule/test/rules.test.ts` (`X251: a schedule that is not one`, from `six fields, naming the five a cron expression has` to `the plugin's own timezone`); `packages/runtime/test/sabotage-schedule.test.ts` (`sabotage: a schedule that is not one -- X251`)

## Refuses when

A scheduled trigger's settings do not say one schedule, or the plugin's own settings are out of range. A trigger of
kind `@schedule/schedule.trigger-kind.json` names exactly one of `cron` and `everyMs` (refused at `settings/cron` when
both, at `settings` when neither). A `cron` is five fields that parse, in a `timezone` this runtime knows; macros such
as `@daily` and a seconds field are not taken. An `everyMs` is a whole number of 1000 or more, since a tick falls on
its multiples since the Unix epoch, and names no `timezone`, since those multiples are the same instants in every
zone. In `project.json`, the plugin's `leaseTtlMs` is a whole number of 1000 or more and its `timezone` a known one,
refused at `plugins/@schedule/settings/<name>`.

## Example

Plant a nightly trigger in the hello feature with a minute out of range:

```
X251  @features/hello/edge/nightly-greeting.trigger.json#settings/cron
    '61' is out of range for minute: 0-59
    → write five fields (for seconds, use everyMs), e.g. "0 3 * * *" for 03:00 every day
```

Six fields read `'0 3 * * * *' has 6 fields; a cron expression has five: minute hour day-of-month month
day-of-week`; an interval of 500 reads `everyMs is 500; a tick is every multiple of it since the Unix epoch, so it is a
whole number of 1000 or more`.

## Fix

Follow the hint for the setting `at` names: a five-field cron expression or an interval of whole milliseconds of 1000
or more, one of the two and not both; an IANA zone for a cron expression and none for an interval; a `leaseTtlMs` of
1000 or more, or none for the default of 30000.
