# T005

- **Family:** T -- triggers
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/triggers.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/runtime/test/sabotage.test.ts` (`T005 a refusal reason the trigger can reach but does not map`)

## Refuses when

A trigger can reach a refusal reason that the refusal table its kind keeps in `settings` does not map. The reasons are
gathered under every profile from what the trigger fires (through maps and bindings, and the `invariant` of a guard
the compiler lowers), from the policies it attaches, and from the guard that identifies its caller. A graph says why
it refused in one word and the trigger says how that word is answered, so every word it can meet needs an answer.
The refusal sits at the table (for http, `settings/response/refusals`) and names the document that refuses.

## Example

Delete `missing` from the get route's table while `get-row` still refuses with it:

```
T005  @features/customers/edge/get-customer.trigger.json#settings/response/refusals
    @features/customers/data/get-row.graph.json may refuse with reason 'missing', which settings.response.refusals does not map
    → add "missing" under settings.response.refusals: how this trigger answers that outcome
```

## Fix

Add the reason to the table with the answer the kind gives it; for http, a status, as in `"missing": 404`. If the
reason is misspelled where it is refused, correct that document rather than mapping the misspelling.
