# A001

- **Family:** A -- access
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/access.ts`
- **Proved by:** `libraries/access/test/access.test.ts` (`A001 a policy whose input reads outside the context, or does not fit the decision under the trigger's kind`, `A001 a decision reading the retired root, told what to write instead; D001 a proof spelt that way`, `A001 at every policy attachment reading the caller, once Principal no longer declares the claims the guard hands`)

## Refuses when

A policy's input is not what its decision can take. Judged on the policy alone: `decide.in` reads something other
than `context.*` (at `decide/in`); `decide.in` is given to an operation that takes no input, or is missing for one
that takes one (at `decide/in` or `decide`); or an entry of `proves` is not a `context.*` path the guard or some kind
hands (at `proves/<index>`). Judged again where a trigger attaches the policy, at `policies/<index>` of the trigger,
since each kind hands a different context: `decide.in` must read what that kind hands, and what it reads must fit the
operation's `accepts`. That fit is strict, where a trigger's `fire.in` is wire-loose: a read that may be missing, such
as the principal of an anonymous caller, feeds only an input the operation declares `required: false`.

## Example

Have `signed-in` read its principal from `{{in.principal}}`. The policy is refused where it is written, and once more
at every trigger that attaches it:

```
A001  @features/access/edge/signed-in.policy.json#decide/in
    decide.in reads 'in', but a policy's input reads context.* only
    → read what the kind and the guard hand: context.principal, context.session, context.challenge, context.headers...
A001  @features/access/edge/get-preferences.trigger.json#policies/0
    policy '@features/access/edge/signed-in.policy.json' under kind '@http/http.trigger-kind.json': principal: 'in': a policy's input reads context.* only
    → wilanis describe @http/http.trigger-kind.json shows what this kind hands
```

Make `requireSignedIn`'s `principal` required instead, and the read of an optional principal no longer fits:

```
A001  @features/access/edge/get-preferences.trigger.json#policies/0
    policy '@features/access/edge/signed-in.policy.json' under kind '@http/http.trigger-kind.json': decide.in → @access/domain/access.port.json#requireSignedIn: field 'principal' is optional but required here
    → a read that may be missing (an anonymous caller) feeds an input the operation declares required: false, and the graph decides on has(...)
```

## Fix

Read the context only, and only what the attaching trigger's kind hands (`wilanis describe` on the kind). Where the
read may be missing, declare the operation's input `required: false` and let the graph decide on `has(...)`: that an
anonymous caller has no principal is what the policy is there to decide. Write `proves` as the `context.*` paths the
guard hands once the policy allows.
