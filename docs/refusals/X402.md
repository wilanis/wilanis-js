# X402

- **Family:** X -- plugin rules, @queue (@wilanis/plugin-queue)
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/plugin-queue/src/rules.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/plugin-queue/test/rules.test.ts` (`X402: a retry on a broker that delivers at most once`: `an outcome that retries, and onFault absent, which retries too`, `a profile that stands a redelivering broker in with one that does not`)

## Refuses when

A queue trigger asks for a retry of a broker that will never deliver the message again. The trigger's connection is of
a kind that declares `"delivery": "at-most-once"` -- under the tree itself, or under a profile that stands another
connection in for it -- and one of its `outcomes` is `retry` (refused at `settings/outcomes/<reason>`), or its
`onFault` is `retry` or absent, which means `retry` (refused at `settings/onFault`). A retry there is a message
dropped while the log says it will be tried again.

## Example

Point the removals trigger at a connection whose kind delivers at most once:

```
X402  @features/customers/edge/removals.trigger.json#settings/outcomes/upstream
    '@connections/once.connection.json' is of kind '@fake-broker/once.connection-kind.json', which delivers at most once, so a message asked to be retried is never delivered again
    → a broker that delivers at most once cannot retry; map the reason to ack or dead, and set onFault
```

The same trigger is refused at `settings/onFault` too, since it leaves `onFault` at its default: the message ends
`(onFault is retry unless it says dead)`.

## Fix

Map every reason to `ack` or `dead` and set `"onFault": "dead"`, or receive from a connection whose kind delivers at
least once, where a retry is a delivery. Where only a profile stands the at-most-once broker in, the fix may instead be
that profile's `connections`.
