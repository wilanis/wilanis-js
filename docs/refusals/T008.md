# T008

- **Family:** T -- triggers
- **Status:** live, since 0.1.0
- **Made in:** `packages/compiler/src/check/triggers.ts`
- **Proved by:** `packages/runtime/test/sabotage-limits.test.ts` (`refuses an unbounded list once, at the trigger's in, naming the field and the shape`)

## Refuses when

A trigger with no policies takes a list with no `maxItems` in one of its edge shapes: its `in`, or a setting its kind
types as `type`, searched to any depth (a nested field is named by its dotted path). A trigger with no policies is
called by anyone, and an unbounded list from an anonymous caller is the request-shaped denial of service; the edge
judges `maxItems` before anything fires. A shape named twice, as a route's body and its `in`, is refused once, where
it is written first. A trigger that attaches any policy is not held to this.

## Example

Make the batch delete public, dropping its policies and the reasons only they reached, and take `maxItems` off its
`ids`:

```
T008  @features/customers/edge/delete-customers.trigger.json#in
    public trigger takes '@customers/edge/DeleteRequest.shape.json', whose field 'ids' is a list with no maxItems
    → add "maxItems" to ids in DeleteRequest.shape.json: the most an anonymous caller may send; or gate the trigger with a policy
```

## Fix

Add `"maxItems"` to the field in the shape the hint names: the most an anonymous caller may send at once. If the
trigger is not meant to be public, attach the policy it should have.
